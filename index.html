What We Treat

Our Speciality Services

We offer all speciality ophthalmology services with the latest techniques and technology.

🔬
Cataract Surgery
Advanced phacoemulsification and IOL implantation for restoring clear vision with minimal recovery time.
✨
LASIK & Refractive Surgery
Freedom from glasses and contact lenses with precise laser correction for myopia, hyperopia, and astigmatism.
🫀
Glaucoma Management
Early detection and treatment of glaucoma to prevent vision loss and preserve long-term eye health.
👁️
Retina Services
Diagnosis and treatment of diabetic retinopathy, macular degeneration, and retinal detachments.
🧒
Pediatric Eye Care
Specialized eye examinations and treatments for children including squint correction and amblyopia therapy.
👓
Optical & Contact Lenses
Complete eye examination with prescription glasses, contact lenses, and low vision aids.
💧
Dry Eye Treatment
Comprehensive evaluation and management of dry eye syndrome for comfortable, healthy vision.
🔭
Squint (Strabismus)
Diagnosis and surgical/non-surgical correction of squint in children and adults.
🩺
Routine Eye Checkup
Comprehensive eye examination for vision screening, spectacle power check, and general eye health assessment.
